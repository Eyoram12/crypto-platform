Frequently Asked Questions

Important information about the CryptoVault demonstration.

Is CryptoVault a real cryptocurrency exchange?

No. This version is a front-end demonstration website hosted with GitHub Pages.

Are the portfolio balances real?

No. Portfolio balances and transactions shown in the dashboard are demonstration data.

Are the market prices live?

The market section retrieves public cryptocurrency market data from Binance's public market-data service.

Can I send real cryptocurrency to the website?

No. Do not send cryptocurrency to any address displayed by this demonstration website.
