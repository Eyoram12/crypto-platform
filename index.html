Frequently Asked Questions

Important information about this demonstration website.

Is CryptoVault a real crypto exchange?

No. CryptoVault is a demonstration website and does not operate as a real cryptocurrency exchange.

Are the portfolio balances real?

No. Portfolio balances and transactions displayed on the website are demonstration data.

Are market prices real?

The market section can display current public market information, but it does not provide trading or investment services.

Can I make a real deposit or withdrawal?

No. Deposits and withdrawals are simulated only. The website does not connect to blockchain wallets, banks or payment processors.

Does the website provide financial advice?

No. CryptoVault is for demonstration and educational purposes and should not be considered financial advice.
