Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about the CryptoVault demonstration.

Is CryptoVault a real cryptocurrency exchange?

No. CryptoVault is currently a demonstration website. It does not process real cryptocurrency deposits, withdrawals or investments.

Are the portfolio balances real?

No. The balances, assets and transactions shown throughout the website are demonstration data.

Does the market section use live information?

The market section retrieves publicly available cryptocurrency market information. Prices change continuously.

Can I create a real account?

No. The registration interface is currently for demonstration purposes and does not create a real financial account.

Are deposits and withdrawals real?

No. The deposit and withdrawal pages are interface demonstrations. No cryptocurrency or money is transferred.
