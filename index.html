Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about the CryptoVault demonstration platform.

Is CryptoVault a real cryptocurrency exchange?

No. This website is a front-end demonstration. It does not process real cryptocurrency deposits, withdrawals or blockchain transactions.

Are the balances real?

No. Portfolio balances and transaction records shown on the website are demonstration data.

Are the market prices real?

The market section displays public market information retrieved from a public market-data API. Market information can change at any time.

Can I use a real wallet address?

No. Do not enter real wallet credentials or send real cryptocurrency through this demonstration site.
